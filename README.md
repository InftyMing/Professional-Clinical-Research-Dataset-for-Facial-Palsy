# Professional-Clinical-Research-Dataset-for-Facial-Palsy


Facial palsy is a common neurological disorder that seriously affects patients' facial appearance and quality of life. Clinical research on facial palsy relies heavily on high-quality, standardized clinical data. This professional clinical research dataset for facial palsy is constructed based on multi-center clinical resources, aiming to provide reliable data support for the diagnosis, treatment, prognosis evaluation and pathogenic mechanism research of facial palsy. 
The dataset includes clinical cases of facial palsy patients collected from medical institutions from 2013 to 2023, covering patients of different ages, genders, and etiological types (such as idiopathic facial palsy, traumatic facial palsy, and infectious facial palsy). The data content comprehensively involves basic demographic information of patients (age, gender, nationality, etc.), detailed clinical symptoms and signs (onset time, affected side, degree of facial paralysis, accompanying symptoms, etc.), results of auxiliary examinations (electroneurography, electromyography, imaging examinations, laboratory tests, etc.), treatment plans (drug therapy, surgical treatment, rehabilitation intervention, etc.), and long-term follow-up data (recovery status, complications, quality of life score, etc.). 

All data in the dataset have undergone strict quality control, including standardized collection of original data, double verification of professional medical personnel, and elimination of invalid and duplicate data, ensuring the accuracy, completeness and reliability of the data. This dataset can be used by researchers in the fields of neurology, rehabilitation medicine, plastic surgery and medical informatics for a series of clinical studies, such as exploring the risk factors of facial palsy, evaluating the efficacy of different treatment methods, establishing prognostic prediction models, and developing intelligent auxiliary diagnosis systems. It is expected to promote the in-depth development of facial palsy clinical research and provide a scientific basis for optimizing clinical diagnosis and treatment strategies. 

Keywords: facial palsy; clinical research; dataset; quality control; follow-up data
